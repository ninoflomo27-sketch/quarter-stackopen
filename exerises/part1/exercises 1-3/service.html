Aura Photography Studio

Aura Photography Studio offers portrait photography, event photography, and other photography services in The Gambia. We aim to capture beautiful moments and create lasting memories for our clients.

Our Services

We provide photography services for individuals, families, and special occasions at affordable prices.

Portrait Photography

We take individual and family portraits for birthdays, personal collections, and social media profiles.

  • Individual portrait session — GMD 300
  • Family portrait session — GMD 500
  • Birthday portraits — GMD 500

Event Photography

We capture important moments at celebrations and special events. Prices depend on the length and size of the event.

  • Small birthday event — GMD 1,500
  • Engagement photography — GMD 2,000
  • Wedding photography — from GMD 5,000

Photography Packages

Clients can choose a package based on their photography needs and budget.

Basic Package

The Basic Package costs GMD 500 and includes a short portrait session with five edited digital photographs.

Premium Package

The Premium Package costs GMD 1,000 and includes a longer portrait session with ten edited digital photographs.

Additional Services

Clients can request extra services to make their photography sessions more suitable for their needs.

  • Photo editing
    • Brightness and colour correction
    • Background adjustments
    • Skin tone correction
  • Extra edited photograph — GMD 50 per photo
  • Printed photograph — price depends on size

How to Book

Follow these steps to arrange a photography session with Aura Photography Studio.

  1. Choose the photography service you need.
  2. Contact the studio to discuss your preferred date and time.
  3. Confirm the price and booking details.
  4. Attend your session or event at the agreed time.

Our Opening Hours

Contact the studio to confirm availability before visiting. Sessions outside normal hours may be arranged by appointment.

  • Monday to Friday: 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM
  • Saturday: 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM
  • Sunday: By appointment
