Power Outage Safety Instructions

These instructions are for people at home or work when electricity goes off and when power returns.

What you need

  • A working flashlight
  • Spare batteries
  • A battery-powered radio
  • A fully charged mobile phone
  • PPE if required for your work

Steps

  1. Stay calm and use a flashlight instead of a candle.
  2. Switch off sensitive electrical equipment.
  3. Keep away from fallen power lines and damaged electrical cables.
  4. Wait for electricity to return and check that the area is safe.
  5. Turn appliances back on one at a time.
  6. Check important equipment to make sure it is working properly.

If something goes wrong

If you see sparks, smoke, or damaged electrical wiring, stay away and contact the appropriate emergency service or qualified electrician. Never touch exposed wires, even if the electricity appears to be off.

Wait until the area is confirmed safe before approaching damaged electrical equipment. Never assume that a power line is safe to touch.
